About

Hello, I'm Cha Beomseok — an electronic music artist and audio-visual creator. My work explores the intersection of soundscapes and visual narratives, often delving into experimental territory.

Using Max/MSP, TouchDesigner and Ableton Live, I build immersive experiences that challenge conventional perceptions of sound and vision — driven by curiosity about how the two mediums coalesce to evoke a shared emotional response.
